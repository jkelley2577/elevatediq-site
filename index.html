Maintenance Agreement – 123 Main St.

Overall rating: Fair (B-). The contract covers basic preventative maintenance but includes several unfavorable clauses and missing performance metrics.

✓ Solid response time language ⚠ Auto-renewal with steep escalations ⚠ No uptime or callback KPIs ℹ Limited door operator coverage

Coverage Gaps

  • No clear inclusion of major components.
  • Ambiguous language around overtime charges.
  • Fire service testing billed separately.

Pricing & Terms

  • Price is ~18% above local benchmarks.
  • Automatic 10% annual increase after year 1.
  • 60-day notice required to cancel.

Recommendation: Renegotiate pricing, cap rate increases at 3–4% annually, add uptime and callback KPIs, and clarify major component coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Can this really replace a human consultant?

ElevatedIQ automates the most time-consuming and repeatable parts of elevator consulting: contract review, scope creation, pricing comparisons, and lifecycle planning. Many customers use it instead of a consultant for everyday decisions, and alongside a consultant for high-stakes projects.

Is my data and documentation secure?

Yes. Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. You choose how long files are retained, and you can request automatic deletion after analysis if required by your internal policies.

Does ElevatedIQ work with all elevator brands?

ElevatedIQ is designed to work across major OEMs and many independents. Our analysis focuses on contract language, pricing, component types, and lifecycle—not just brand-specific marketing terms.

What if my contracts are old scans or hard to read?

In many cases, we can still extract enough information to provide helpful guidance. For extremely low-quality scans, you may need to upload a cleaner copy or key terms manually.

Can I share reports with owners, boards, or leadership?

Absolutely. Every analysis includes a clear, sharable summary and optional PDF export designed for non-technical stakeholders.

How do we get started with a portfolio rollout?

You can begin with one or two buildings, then expand. For larger portfolios, we’ll work with you to structure plans, onboarding, and training so your team sees value quickly.
